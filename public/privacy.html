Privacy Policy

by Other Desk Platform, operated by By Other Construction (Florida). Last updated November 11, 2026.

What we collect

The platform holds the business records By Other Construction needs: projects, bids, invoices, costs, payments, documents, subcontractor and vendor details, and time-clock records for its crew. For people with accounts it holds a name, work email address, and role.

QuickBooks Online

When the company's administrator connects QuickBooks, the platform reads (it never changes) invoices, payments, bills and expense transactions so they can be matched to projects and job costs. The connection uses Intuit's secure sign-in; the platform never sees or stores the QuickBooks password. The access token is stored encrypted on the company's server. Administrators can disconnect at any time, which stops all further reading, and can also revoke the app in QuickBooks.

Google and Microsoft

Sign-in with Google confirms a person's company email address. The platform can read a mailbox label of bid invitations and store job files in the company's SharePoint, only with the administrator's approval.

How we use and share it

Data is used only to run the company's own business. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside the company except service providers that host the platform and its database, and where the law requires it.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored on managed, access-controlled servers. Sensitive items, such as employee tax forms, are visible only to approved administrators. Business and tax records are kept as long as the law and the company's needs require.

Your choices

Staff and subcontractors may ask the company to correct their information. Administrators can remove integrations and accounts at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
